# LOWSUN — Milestone 0 Playbook

**Purpose:** Complete a one-week evidence and data audit that makes Milestone 1
predictable. M0 is not an implementation sprint.

## M0 in one sentence

Finish the week able to say:

> For this lunar site, these camera calibrations and this real-image sequence
> support this specific measurable comparison; here are the sources, rights,
> formats, dependencies, and decision threshold.

The governing scope and acceptance criteria remain in
[`lowsun-portfolio-rescope.md`](lowsun-portfolio-rescope.md), sections 6 and
9. This document turns them into an ordered working plan.

## Before starting

- Create a working branch if desired, using the `codex/` prefix.
- Create `PLAN_DOCS/notes/` for source notes.
- Keep a dated decision log. Record uncertainty rather than quietly resolving
  it in code.
- Do not begin camera, renderer, terrain-ingest, or label-parser
  implementation during M0. Inspect representative files and labels only.

## Day 1 — Reorient around the project question

Read these sections of `lowsun-portfolio-rescope.md`:

1. Executive summary (section 1).
2. Problem statement and objectives (sections 3–4).
3. CAHVORE non-centrality study (sections 6.1–6.3).
4. Milestone 0 and dependency decisions (sections 9–9.0.1).

Write `PLAN_DOCS/m0-charter.md`, limited to one page, in your own words. It
should capture:

- The two questions LOWSUN will answer:
  1. When is a central camera approximation inaccurate enough to matter?
  2. Does that difference affect a real downstream vision task?
- The M1 comparison paths:
  1. True CAHVORE rays as the reference.
  2. A fitted central CAHVOR model plus resampling.
  3. A naive pinhole baseline.
- An initial operating envelope: lunar surface and/or descent imagery. Leave
  exact ranges open until candidate data establishes a realistic envelope.
- The key guardrail: a non-zero `E` vector does **not** demonstrate
  non-central behavior. CAHVORE type `T` is required, and type 3 additionally
  requires its linearity parameter `P`.

The central concept to regain before proceeding is the distinction between:

- **Lens distortion:** range-independent image warping, which a central
  render-and-resample path can reproduce; and
- **Non-centrality:** field-angle-dependent ray origins, which cause
  range-dependent parallax and require per-pixel ray origins for exact
  rendering.

## Day 2 — Build the primary-source reading path

Read the primary reference already included with the project:

- `PLAN_DOCS/whitepapers/Generalized_Camera_Calibration_including_Fish-Eye_Lenses.pdf`

Read strategically rather than linearly. In
`PLAN_DOCS/notes/cahvore-primary-source.md`, make notes with page references
for:

- The meanings of `C`, `A`, `H`, `V`, `O`, `R`, and `E`.
- The behavior of CAHVORE types 1, 2, and 3.
- The role of `P` for type 3.
- Forward projection versus inverse projection/ray generation.
- Coordinate-frame conventions and units.
- Numerical or edge-of-field caveats.

Also locate the 2001 Gennery paper named in section 9.0.1 and the relevant
PDS geometric-camera-model label documentation. The objective is to identify
the authoritative source for every field the project will later parse or
implement.

## Day 3 — Gather camera candidates before selecting one

Create `PLAN_DOCS/m0-camera-candidates.md`. Record two or three public camera
calibrations. Prefer candidates with meaningful **type-appropriate**
non-centrality and nearby/associated public imagery that could support the
later real-data evaluation.

For each candidate, record:

| Field | What to capture |
| --- | --- |
| Identity | Camera, instrument, image/product ID, and stable archive URL or identifier |
| Calibration | `C A H V O R E`, `T`, `P`, image dimensions, units, and frames |
| Interpretation | Plain-language expected entrance-pupil behavior based on actual `T` and `P` |
| Product details | Format, label structure, and plausible parsing approach |
| Rights | Usage terms, required attribution, and redistribution limits |
| Real-data link | Associated sequence and why it may support a downstream task |

Selection rule:

- Prefer a candidate with meaningful non-centrality **given its type** and
  usable real imagery.
- If no candidate supports that claim, document the negative finding. Pivot M1
  toward distortion/resampling accuracy rather than forcing a non-centrality
  conclusion.

Do not choose a camera based on `E` alone, and do not download terrain tiles
or write parsers yet.

## Day 4 — Select the site, real benchmark, and metric together

Choose a lunar site and terrain product only after verifying they can support
the future comparison.

For the selected terrain product, record:

- Citation, archive location, access date, and usage terms.
- Spatial resolution, coverage, and file format.
- CRS/body-fixed frame, datum, vertical convention, and units.
- Whether coverage and resolution plausibly match candidate image geometry.
- The expected reader/tooling to evaluate later.

For the real evaluation sequence, record:

- Image IDs and sources of pose and calibration.
- Approximate overlap with the terrain site and illumination regime.
- Whether available pose/ground truth is sufficient for the proposed task.
- Material limitations: map mismatch, unknown processing, exposure, timing,
  terrain change, and missing metadata.

Choose one initial downstream metric. Start with feature
matching/correspondence error unless the selected sequence provides credible
enough pose and map support for map-relative localization. Localization can be
the preferred extension; it need not block M0.

Write the operational question in `PLAN_DOCS/m0-benchmark-definition.md`, for
example:

> Under the selected range and field-angle envelope, does the central
> approximation introduce enough correspondence error to exceed the tolerance
> of the chosen detector or matcher?

Record a pre-results decision threshold. `0.05 px` may be used provisionally
only if the document also states how it will be justified or revised using the
actual detector, matcher, and image scale.

## Day 5 — Make the decisions reproducible

Complete these artifacts:

### `PLAN_DOCS/m0-data-rights-ledger.md`

For every terrain, calibration, image sequence, paper, and external product,
record the source, persistent identifier/URL, access date, citation, licence
or terms, required attribution, redistribution status, local format, and any
constraints.

### `PLAN_DOCS/m0-dependency-decision-record.md`

Apply the M0 rule: reimplement what is the contribution; depend on or vendor
what is incidental.

Record these initially proposed decisions and the evidence behind each:

- Reimplement CAHV/CAHVOR/CAHVORE projection and ray generation from primary
  sources.
- Evaluate an existing PDS calibration-label parser before writing one.
- Evaluate and verify a CAHVOR conversion implementation before depending on
  or vendoring it.
- Locate an independent verification oracle, ideally an open JPL `cmod`
  release; third-party code is a secondary cross-check, not the primary
  implementation.

For every external dependency, record its licence, maintenance status, exact
function, validation plan, and fallback if it is incorrect or abandoned.

### `PLAN_DOCS/m0-benchmark-definition.md`

Record the selected site, terrain product, calibration(s), real sequence,
task, metric, range envelope, field-angle samples, and preregistered
threshold.

### `PLAN_DOCS/m0-decision-log.md`

Add dated decisions, assumptions, evidence, unresolved questions, and the
next action that would resolve each question.

Finally, order the reference detector for the Milestone 3b sensor-validation
work, if that milestone remains in scope. M0 authorizes acquisition only; it
does not include detector characterization.

## Exit checklist

M0 is complete, and M1 may begin, only when all of these are true:

- [ ] One lunar site and terrain product are selected; their frames, units,
      format, and rights are understood.
- [ ] Two or three camera candidates are recorded with complete CAHVORE
      metadata, especially `T` and `P`.
- [ ] At least one selected calibration has meaningful non-centrality for its
      specific type, or the negative finding is recorded explicitly.
- [ ] A real sequence has been inspected sufficiently to make the later
      benchmark plausible.
- [ ] One task, metric, operating envelope, and pre-results decision threshold
      are documented.
- [ ] Every input has a stable citation, known format, and documented usage
      terms.
- [ ] Dependency roles, licences, validation oracles, and fallbacks are
      recorded.
- [ ] The reference detector has been ordered, if M3b remains in scope.
- [ ] No renderer, terrain, or camera implementation has been selected merely
      from intuition; those choices await the M1 decision study.

## What comes next

Milestone 1 begins only after this audit. Its first task is not renderer
integration: implement and validate the CAHV/CAHVOR/CAHVORE projection and ray
generation path in float64 from primary sources, then measure the numerical
error floor before evaluating the central approximation.
